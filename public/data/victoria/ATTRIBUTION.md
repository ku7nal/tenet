# Victoria storm-drain GIS attribution

Source: City of Victoria, [Storm Drain Infrastructure service](https://maps.victoria.ca/server/rest/services/OpenData/OpenData_StormDrain/MapServer), including gravity mains, manholes, outfalls, and catchment areas. The public GeoJSON files are a saved and transformed snapshot of this source.

The City of Victoria [Open Government Licence](https://opendata.victoria.ca/pages/open-data-licence) permits reuse of information covered by the licence for lawful purposes, including copying, modifying, publishing, adapting, and distributing it. The required fallback attribution is:

> Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – City of Victoria.

The source service's layer copyright fields are blank. Confirm the snapshot layers are City information covered by the licence and contain no third-party material before redistributing them. The City's GIS is not authoritative for engineering.
